Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Figra (the “Add-in”) collects and uses information.

Registration and purpose

Figra requires account registration to support ongoing development and research activities. The information you provide is collected as part of the development outcomes of this project and is used to better understand how Figra is used in educational and research contexts.

We may reference aggregated and anonymized information to improve documentation, educational materials, and future features, and to support scientific research and publications related to research community practices.

As the Figra user community grows, we may offer an optional mailing list or community channel for researchers and educators to share updates, research-related information, and best practices. Participation in such communications will always be optional.

Information we collect

When you register, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Job title or role
  • Affiliation or organization
  • Country

When you participate in an optional in-app survey, we collect:

  • Your responses to the survey questions
  • No personally identifiable information is required unless you choose to provide it

Figra operates entirely in your browser. We do not collect usage data, error logs, or any information about your spreadsheet content. Excel data is processed locally and never transmitted to the developer.

Optional surveys

From time to time, Figra may display optional, voluntary questionnaires within the add-in. These surveys are designed to gather community feedback and may be initiated by community members or the developer. Participation is entirely optional — skipping a survey will not affect your access to Figra in any way. Responses are collected anonymously and may be used in aggregated form for scientific research, publications, or studies of the research community.

How we use information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide and maintain the Add-in
  • To send service-related messages and product updates
  • To improve features, usability, and reliability
  • To support scientific research and academic publications related to research community practices
  • To comply with legal obligations

Updates and communications

We may send product updates, announcements, or information related to Figra development. In the future, these communications may include information about related tools, events, or services relevant to research and educational communities.

Sharing and third parties

Registration and survey responses may be submitted via Google Forms, a third-party service operated by Google LLC. Such submissions are subject to Google's privacy policies and terms. The developer accesses this data solely for development, research insights, and improvement of Figra.

We do not sell or trade your personal information. Information may only be disclosed to other parties if required to comply with applicable laws or legal obligations.

Data retention

Registration information is retained indefinitely unless you request deletion. Survey responses are retained as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy.

Your choices

  • You may request deletion of your registration information by contacting us via the Support page.
  • Participation in surveys is always optional and can be skipped at any time.
  • You may opt out of optional communications (for example, community mailing lists) where applicable.

Contact

For privacy-related requests or questions, please contact us via the Support page: Support
